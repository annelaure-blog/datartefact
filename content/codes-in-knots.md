description: 'A suspended series of carved wooden knots, each form standing in for
  a word — verb or noun — in an invented notation system built from knot shapes
  rather than characters. The project draws a direct line to khipu and other precolonial
  textile-based recording systems, translating their logic of knotted encoding into
  a contemporary sculptural alphabet. Shown in the exhibition "Digital Decoding:
  Digitality as Subject and Method" at Silent Green Kunstquartier, Berlin.'
