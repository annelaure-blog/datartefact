author: Aurignacian cave visitors
description: Roughly 100 ochre palm-prints applied by hand in the Brunel Chamber of Chauvet cave, forming a large shape suggestive of a herbivore. Oliver Schlaudt cites the panel's repetition of an identical gesture as a case where repeated mark-making can drift toward tokenization, independent of any single mark's meaning.
