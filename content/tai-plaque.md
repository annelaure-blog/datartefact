---
title: The Taï Plaque
date: c. 12,000 BP
author: Late Magdalenian engravers
description: An engraved bovine rib fragment from the Grotte du Taï, France, bearing
  over a thousand tiny incisions on one face — among the densest mark-sequences known
  from the end of the Upper Palaeolithic, and, like the notched bones of Abri Cellier
  and La Marche, read within the broader debate over Palaeolithic notational systems.
