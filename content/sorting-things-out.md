**Sorting Things Out: Classification and Its Consequences** by Geoffrey C. Bowker and Susan Leigh Star (1999).

A classic in science and technology studies. The central argument: every classification system encodes values and power structures, and the act of classifying always has consequences for what becomes visible or invisible.

Highly relevant to anyone thinking seriously about data collection and categorisation.
