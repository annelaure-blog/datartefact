title: Abri Cellier Notched Bones
date: c. 32,000 BCE
author: Aurignacian toolmakers
description: Two bone fragments from the Abri Cellier rock shelter in the Dordogne,
  France, each carved along one edge with a long, regular series of parallel notches.
  Excavated in 1927 by the Logan Museum of Anthropology's George Collie, Abri Cellier
  is one of several Aurignacian sites in southwest France — alongside Abri Blanchard
  and La Marche — whose notched bones have been read as early notational or tallying
  sequences.
