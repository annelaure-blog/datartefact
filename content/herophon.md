title: Herophon Disc
date: '1880'
author: Ch. F. Pietschmann & Söhne
description: A square punched cardboard disc for the Herophon, a hand-cranked organette
  introduced in Germany in the 1880s. Unlike most organettes, the disc stays stationary
  while the 24-note reed mechanism revolves beneath it, reading the pattern of holes
  as pre-programmed tunes.
