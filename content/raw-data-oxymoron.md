**Raw Data Is an Oxymoron**, edited by Lisa Gitelman (MIT Press, 2013).

The title says it all. Each essay in this collection examines a different moment in the history of data collection — from 19th-century actuarial tables to early internet packet logs — and shows how every dataset is the product of choices, instruments, and institutions.

The introduction alone ("Data Bite Man") is worth the read and is freely available online.

A foundational companion to *Sorting Things Out*.
