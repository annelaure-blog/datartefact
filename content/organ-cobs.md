title: Organ Cobs
date: '1880'
author: Autophone Company
description: Organ cobs or rollers were used in roller organs, and consisted of
  a cylinder of wood with pins in them that pressed on the keys in the organ to
  actuate them. Roller organs were a type of reed organ introduced in the late
  1880s by the Autophone company of New York, and were an inexpensive and popular
  means of entertainment for the US market.
