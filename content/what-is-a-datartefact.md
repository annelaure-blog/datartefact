A **datartefact** is any object, document, or artefact that carries data — intentionally or not.

## Why it matters

Most data we encounter in the world is embedded in physical or cultural objects rather than spreadsheets. Understanding these objects as data sources changes how we read and interpret them.

## Examples

- A ship's log from the 18th century
- A census record
- A hand-drawn map
- A product label
