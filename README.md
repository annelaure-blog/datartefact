# DATARTEFACT — Research & Presentations Archive

This repository archives presentations, slide decks, draft texts, diagrams, and working documents related to the **DATARTEFACT** book series.

The project investigates the art, design, and history of physical artefacts encoding data, with a particular focus on their ontological status as material documents.

---

## About the Project

**DATARTEFACT** is an editorial research project exploring data physicalization through both empirical case studies and theoretical inquiry.

The core hypothesis guiding Issue 1:

> Datartefacts are physical objects that function as documents — stabilizing and making inspectable relational traces of the world.

The research develops:
- A historical lineage (from scientific inscription systems to contemporary practices)
- A conceptual framework distinguishing documentary objects, documentary apparatuses, and instrumental systems
- A spectrum spanning autographic design, data physicalization, and data visualization
